CONTROLS & BUTTONS

Every input explained. This guide stays below the game and never blocks the action.

🎵 Music + SFX · 🐌 Gary meows · No spoken dialogue

Movement & catching

A / D · ← / →
Move left or right; hold a direction to auto-sprint.
Shift
Sprint immediately while moving. Use D + Shift + Space for a sprint jump.
Space · W · ↑
Jump on land; swim upward underwater; rise when flying.
S · ↓
Swim or fly downward; no movement effect on land.
Hold J / F
Extend the net to catch jellyfish. After Gary joins, he catches behind you.
Hold B / K
Fire bubbles, shrink bosses, and stun electric jellyfish. Gary helps later.
E
Talk to nearby characters, accept quests, and turn in completed quests.
C
Clarinet stun ability; unlock it by finishing Squidward's quest.

Menus & shortcuts

Q · SHOP
Open or close the shop during play. Purchase equipment, boosts, and healing.
L · QUEST LOG
Open or close your current quest objectives and rewards.
P · PAUSE
Pause or resume play. Escape also pauses or resumes.
X · FULL SCREEN
Enter or exit fullscreen. You can also click the fullscreen button.
M · SOUND
Turn game audio on or off: music, effects, and Gary's meow.
H / ? · CONTROLS
Show the in-game controls window; you can also read this guide anytime.
Enter · INTRO
Advance SpongeBob's opening story while an intro page is shown.
LOAD TRACK
Choose a local audio file to replace the built-in looping music.
SWITCH INPUT
Switch between Desktop keyboard controls and Mobile touch controls.

Power-ups · Keys 1–7

1 · MEGA NETTemporarily enlarge your catching net.
2 · FREEZE WAVETemporarily freeze jellyfish and bosses.
3 · STAR SHIELDTemporarily protect SpongeBob from shocks.
4 · JELLY MAGNETDraw nearby jellyfish and pickups closer.
5 · SLOW BUBBLESlow hostile movement.
6 · GOLDEN HOUREarn double coins while active.
7 · CLOUD HOPFloat longer and clear wider gaps.

Power-ups must be found or purchased before their keys can activate them. All game buttons have a specific purpose; unavailable actions are disabled or show their unlock requirement.
